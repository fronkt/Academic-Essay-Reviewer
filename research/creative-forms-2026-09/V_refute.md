# V_refute — adversarial verification of `references/creative_forms.md`

Checked 2026-09-23 against live pages. §7 skipped (placeholder).

**How each source was re-read (on its own, not from the research notes):**
- **Chrome** (own tab; `document.body.innerText`, or a same-origin `fetch()` of each page parsed in the browser). Used for:
  - all 64 JHU `apply.jhu.edu/hopkins-insider` pages and the 8 listing pages
  - all CEG pages cited
  - the Common App help-center and member-center articles, with dates read from the article metadata element
  - Creative Nonfiction's POV page (curl gets a Cloudflare challenge)
  - Harper's
- **Fresh curl.** Every other page was fetched again with my own user agent into `scratchpad/creative/verify/cache/`. I did not use the researchers' `raw/` copies.
  - The page text was stripped and matched in `verify/vcheck.py`. The only normalization is typography: curly→straight quotes, dash variants, ellipsis and whitespace.
  - PDFs were read with pypdf.
  - Tulane and UVA were read through the Blogger JSON feed.
  - UChicago was read through its Gatsby page-data JSON.
- "NORM" in my logs means the only difference was typographic, such as curly vs straight quotes. That counts as CONFIRMED.
