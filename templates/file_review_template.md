
## Rules

- Every line traces to a Phase 1 lane report; every rating names its lane. No invented findings (Checkpoint 3).
- Band caps are arithmetic (Checkpoint 8): unresolved DA CRITICAL inconsistency OR Academic BLOCK → ≤ REACH-PLAUSIBLE.
- The uncertainty caveat is verbatim and inseparable from the band — a band without it is a template violation.
- No percentages, no odds, no "chances" language anywhere in the package.
- NOT PROVIDED sections appear in the coverage matrix as gaps; they are never imputed.
