# Activities & Honors Rubric

Used by `activities_awards_reader_agent` (craft + tiering) and `authenticity_reader_agent` (file-mode inflation checks). Base-rate framing mirrors `cliche_taxonomy.md`: name the pattern, say how often a reader sees it, state the salvage condition.

## EC tier framework (scarcity, not worth)

| Tier | Definition | Examples | Base rate in a T10/T20 pool |
|------|-----------|----------|------------------------------|
| **T1** | National/international distinction with external validation | ISEF grand award, IMO/IOI/IPhO camp+, STS finalist, national team, published first-author research, nationally-charting founder | A handful per reader per season; largely decides the EC rating alone |
| **T2** | State/regional distinction, or founder/creator with real measured traction | State champion, all-state, regional science-fair top prize, founded org with verifiable external users/revenue/press, selective research program with output | Common at the top of the pool; differentiates via trajectory and specificity |
| **T3** | School-level leadership or sustained depth with progression | Club president, varsity captain, section leader, 4-year commitment member→leader, a job with growing responsibility | The pool median's ceiling; earns its keep through description craft and coherence |
| **T4** | Membership/participation without distinct role | Club member, NHS, generic volunteer hours | Fills slots; costs nothing, adds ~nothing; >3 of these in 10 slots is itself a finding |

- Tiers describe **scarcity in the pool**, never the worth of the kid. Context (UC factor 12-style: work obligations, family care, opportunity access) re-tiers: 20 hrs/wk at a family business is T2-equivalent evidence of capacity, and readers who miss that are bad readers.
- **Trajectory beats snapshot**: member→founder→state-recognized across three years outreads a static T2.

## Slot craft (the description as an object)

1. **Verb-first, quantified**: what you did, at what scale, with what result. "Built X used by Y; Z outcome." Zero aspiration language ("hoping to...", "passionate about...") — the slot is for evidence.
2. **Don't restate the fields**: the position and organization boxes already exist; a description that repeats them wastes its only sentence.
3. **Per-portal economy** (`portal_specs.md` limits): Common App 150 chars = one result-bearing sentence, abbreviations acceptable, full sentences not required. UC 350 chars = role sentence + quantified-result sentence, no third. MIT 4 slots = curation IS the content — grade the *selection* ("the four that mean the most") before the wording; a trophy-ordered four that ignores the prompt misreads the form.
4. **Ordering is signaling**: importance order, tier-highest or narrative-central first. An applicant leading with T4 NHS while their T2 research sits sixth is a self-report of not knowing what matters — a roadmap item, not a character flaw.
5. **Hours honesty**: hrs/wk × wks/yr gets sanity-checked (DA lane). Summed claims exceeding a livable week is a credibility hit that bleeds into every other section.
6. **The humanity slot**: one non-strategic entry (a job, caregiving, a real hobby) reads as a person, not a portfolio. Ten optimized slots is its own tell.

## Honors section

- Order by level of recognition (international → national → state → regional → school), then selectivity.
- **Make opaque names legible**: "1 of N from M applicants" inside the 100 chars when the award name carries no information. An unexplained acronym is a wasted slot.
- School-level awards fill remaining slots only; they cannot lead.
- Grade-level and level-of-recognition fields do tiering work — set them accurately; "school" level claimed as "national" is a DA finding.
- Pay-to-play programs (unselective paid summer programs, vanity "honor societies") listed as honors: readers recognize them instantly; each one costs credibility. Salvage condition: none — cut them.

## Spike vs. well-rounded (file-shape read)

- At T10/T20 the admitted shape is overwhelmingly **spike + human**: top slots tell one coherent story, remaining slots prove a person exists around it.
- Coherence test: do the top 5 activities + top 3 honors + essay topics point at the same identity? Scattered excellence reads as "impressive, unclear seat" — the institutional reader's lane.
- Well-rounded-no-spike is a viable flagship/honors shape; presented at T10 it's the pool median and rates accordingly.

## Red-flag patterns (DA cross-checks)

| Pattern | What the reader sees | Salvage |
|---------|---------------------|---------|
| Title inflation ("Founder & CEO", club of 3) | Every other title in the file gets re-read skeptically | Honest title + real numbers outperforms the puffed version |
| Essay↔list contradiction (essay's centerpiece project absent from activities, or scoped differently) | Fabrication question mark over the whole file | Reconcile: add the slot or fix the essay's claim |
| Hour-sum implausibility | Credibility hit, file-wide | Audit and correct before submission |
| Duplicate credit (same project as activity + honor + essay + additional-info) | One achievement wearing four costumes | Once as activity, once as its award — the essay may deepen, never re-list |
| Vanity honors / pay-to-play | Sophistication doubt | Cut |
